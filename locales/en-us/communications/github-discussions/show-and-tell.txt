Show us what you have built with Lily

This category is for sharing what you have made with Lily Design System™: an app, a theme, an integration with another framework, a tutorial, or an experiment. Finished or not, it is welcome here.

## How to share

Copy these headings into a new discussion and fill them in.

- **What it is**: one sentence.
- **Why you made it**: two or three sentences.
- **Something to look at**: a screenshot, a short code sample, or a link to a demo.
- **How to try it**: a few steps, or a link.
- **What was hard or surprising**: one thing.
- **What you would like back**: feedback, contributors, or other people's examples.

## My example

**What it is**: the Lily example apps, one per framework, each showing every component with a complete stylesheet.

**Why I made it**: the headless components ship no CSS, so on their own they look like plain HTML. The example apps show what they look like with real styling, real interactivity, and a working page shell, so you can judge Lily before you commit to it.

**How to try it**: open https://lilydesignsystem.com/examples/ and pick a framework. Each component has its own page with a live demo and its markup.

**What was hard**: keeping the same component behaving the same way in nine frameworks. The source of every app is in the repository: https://github.com/LilyDesignSystem/lily-design-system

**What I would like back**: show yours. Anything built with Lily, however small, helps the next person see what is possible.
