Which screen reader and browser do you or your users rely on most?

Description:

Lily Design System™ has automated keyboard and ARIA tests for its 40 core candidate components, but no screen-reader testing has been recorded yet. The [screen-reader matrix](https://github.com/LilyDesignSystem/lily-design-system/blob/main/docs/screen-reader-matrix.md) needs six combinations filled for each component, and the testing will happen one combination at a time.

This poll decides which combination gets tested first. The result is advisory: the maintainer will start with the most-voted combination, unless the comments give a strong reason to do otherwise. There is no closing date; the first round of testing will use the result as it stands when it starts.

Please vote for the one combination you use most, or that most of your users use. If you can, say why in a comment: whether you use a screen reader yourself, test with one, or know it from your users' data.

Poll question: Which screen reader and browser do you or your users rely on most?

Options:

1. NVDA with Firefox (Windows)
2. NVDA with Chrome (Windows)
3. JAWS with Chrome (Windows)
4. VoiceOver with Safari (macOS)
5. VoiceOver with Safari (iOS)
6. TalkBack with Chrome (Android)
7. Something else (explain in comments)
