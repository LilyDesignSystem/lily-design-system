What do you want more of in Lily?

This poll helps decides which features get created first. The result is advisory: the maintainer will start with the most-voted combination, unless the comments give a strong reason to do otherwise. There is no closing date; the first round of testing will use the result as it stands when it starts.

Please vote for the one combination you use most, or that most of your users use. If you can, say why in a comment: whether you use a screen reader yourself, test with one, or know it from your users' data.

What do you want more of in Lily?

Options:

- Features such as new components
- Platforms such as new systems in addition to React, Vue, Svelte, ec.
- Accessibility such as ARIA, usability, reasonable adjustments, etc.
- Locales such as more languages and geographic regions
- Themes such as more color options
- Examples
- Tutorials
