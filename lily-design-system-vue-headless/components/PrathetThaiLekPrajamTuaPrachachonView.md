# PrathetThaiLekPrajamTuaPrachachonView

A headless Vue 3 read-only display for Thailand's National Identification Number.

Format: 13 digits: registration category, district/province office code, a serial number, a Modulus-11 check digit, and 2 further disambiguating digits.

Companion: `PrathetThaiLekPrajamTuaPrachachonInput`.

References: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_identity_card
