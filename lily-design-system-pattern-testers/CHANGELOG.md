# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — unreleased

- `command`: the command palette's keys.
- `grid-actions`: sorting, editing and selecting by key.
- `menu-submenu` and `menubar-submenu`. `menu`, `menubar` and `tree` gain type-ahead; `menu` and `tree` gain activation.
- `combobox` is the whole pattern now, not a part: arrows, Alt+Arrow, Home, End, Enter, Escape, typing and a click, on three fixture options the tester installs.
- `tabs`, `menu`, `menubar`, `tree`, `grid`, `combobox`, `dialog`, `picker-listbox` and `picker-disclosure` contracts, with a DOM driver and a WebdriverIO runner.
