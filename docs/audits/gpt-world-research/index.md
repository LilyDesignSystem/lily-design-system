# Global healthcare software audit of Lily Design System™

26 November 2026 · Repository baseline `a9761da28`

**Fix the handoff from patient search to the patient summary first.** In the SvelteKit example shipped by Lily™, a clerk can select one person and arrive at another person’s summary. Both records are fictional, but the example teaches a pattern adopters could copy. This is the one finding in this review for which the incorrect output was reproduced.

**Prioritize three examples that follow work across screens.** Trace a referral through discharge, a community visit from offline entry to confirmation, and a population indicator back to its source. Each answers a question that an individual component cannot answer. Who owns the next action? Has the receiving service confirmed the update? Which people does this number represent? Lily already has patient banners, clinical record views, accessible tables, timelines and provenance fields to build on. ## A patient search can open the wrong record

The search result for the synthetic patient **Chidi Okafor, born 12 July 1990**, links to the summary with a fictional NHS number in the query string. The destination initializes to **Alexandra Example, born 3 February 1949**. Its patient switcher and late-result protection operate within the summary page. Neither resolves the person selected on the preceding page. The existing search test stops after checking the matching row. [Search source](../../../lily-design-system-svelte-sveltekit-examples/src/routes/find-patient/+page.svelte) · [Summary source](../../../lily-design-system-svelte-sveltekit-examples/src/routes/patient-summary/+page.svelte) · [Search tests](../../../lily-design-system-svelte-sveltekit-examples/e2e/clinic-pages.spec.ts)

**Verification.** I traced the link in source and requested its destination from the locally built SvelteKit server. The selected identifier, an unknown identifier, and the plain summary URL all returned HTTP 200 with Alexandra’s name and birth date in the patient banner. This check covers the initial server-rendered page. The search source and summary source also use separate fixture lists. Reliable identification across EHR screens is the subject of the US SAFER patient identification guide. [SAFER patient identification](https://healthit.gov/resources/2025-safer-guide-patient-identification/)

**Recommended repair.** Give the example one shared set of synthetic patients. Link with an opaque demo record key and resolve it before rendering any banner or clinical section. For an unresolvable key, show a recovery link to search and withhold patient content. Preserve the switcher’s protection against late results. Add a browser test that follows each matching row, including the two same-name, same-birth-date rows, and compares the destination’s identifiers **and a patient-specific clinical entry** with the selected fixture. Check direct navigation, reload and Back. An opaque key also avoids putting a health identifier in browser history. A production service owns authorization for every record lookup. [Lily privacy guidance](../../../PHI.md) · [Worked wrong-patient hazard](../../assurance/hazard-log-example.md)

## Recommended work and acceptance checks

These priorities reflect the consequence of a misleading display, the strength of the local evidence and how much existing work Lily can reuse. The first three items below can begin with synthetic scenarios in the SvelteKit examples. They call for no new package. Services supply clinical meaning, permissions, state transitions, storage and delivery receipts. Lily supplies the way people perceive and navigate that information.

### 1. Carry responsibility through referral, admission and discharge

Extend the clinic flow board, ward board and handover rehearsal into one illustrated hospital pathway. Begin with a referral returned for more information, then show acceptance, waiting for a bed, transfer and discharge with an outstanding result. At each handoff show the sending and receiving service, who currently owns the next action, the action due, and when the status last changed. A transfer request can coexist with the patient’s current bed. Keep the current location until the service confirms the move. For patient administration teams the distinction matters when two clerks act on the same vacancy. [Flow board](../../../lily-design-system-svelte-sveltekit-examples/src/routes/flow-board/+page.svelte) · [Ward board](../../../lily-design-system-svelte-sveltekit-examples/src/routes/ward-board/+page.svelte) · [Handover rehearsal](../../../lily-design-system-svelte-sveltekit-examples/src/routes/handover/+page.svelte)

In the results inbox, retain “reviewed by” and add a separate follow-up owner, due time and outcome. Use an amended result after discharge as the test case. A bulk review must leave the pending callback visible. If the bed becomes unavailable or the receiving service declines a request, a keyboard user should be able to identify the current location, the responsible team and the next action after refresh. Simulate these responses locally. The host resolves reservations and duplicate submissions. An adopter using FHIR R4 can map the requested service to `ServiceRequest` and its fulfilment to `Task`. [Lily results inbox](../../../lily-design-system-svelte-sveltekit-examples/src/routes/results-inbox/+page.svelte) · [HL7 ServiceRequest](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/servicerequest.html) · [HL7 Task](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/task.html)

For a **medicines reconciliation** variant, compare a prior medication list, the inpatient administration chart and the proposed discharge list. The existing chart distinguishes product strength, prescribed dose and amount given. Preserve those distinctions and label each source, time and discrepancy awaiting review. Use a fixture in which a dose was omitted on the ward but the prescription remains active. The discharge view must preserve both facts and leave the reconciliation decision with the clinician. [Lily medicines administration example](../../../lily-design-system-svelte-sveltekit-examples/src/routes/medicines-administration/+page.svelte) · [WHO medication safety in transitions of care](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-UHC-SDS-2019.9)

### 2. Rehearse a community visit through interrupted synchronization

The provenance view already has words for work saved on the device and for a failed send. Use it in a community visit list with the time observed, time recorded, last server confirmation and an action to resolve a conflict. Include a visit entered during a home call while another team corrects the same record at the clinic. Provide a read-only text comparison that identifies which version came from whom. The host controls persistence, retries and clinical reconciliation. [Record provenance contract](../../../components/record-provenance-view/index.md) · [Lily privacy boundary](../../../PHI.md) · [CHT offline design](https://docs.communityhealthtoolkit.org/technical-overview/concepts/offline-first/)

**Acceptance check.** Simulate a lost acknowledgment, reconnect, then retry. The worker can identify the unsent or unconfirmed visit throughout. Only a receipt for that visit changes its state to received. A late receipt for the previous patient cannot complete the new patient’s visit. Show the conflict until the host confirms its resolution. Exercise the example on a narrow screen, with the keyboard and with a screen reader.

### 3. Give population indicators a denominator people can inspect

Build one district immunization coverage example for an analyst and one permission-limited follow-up view for a community team. Start with Lily’s existing dashboard rule to show the denominator. Add its definition, source, reporting period, geography, reporting completeness and last update. Show the calculation’s inputs in an accessible table beside the chart. Make individual drill-down conditional on the host’s authorization and the availability of a linked cohort. A census projection cannot yield a list of named eligible people. The WHO antenatal digital adaptation kit also connects service workflows, data elements and programme indicators, a useful model for documenting the path from a visit to a report. [Lily dashboard guidance](../../patterns/healthcare/index.md#dashboards) · [Rwanda registry](https://dhis2.org/rwanda-crvs-eir-integration/) · [WHO digital adaptation kit](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240020306)

**Acceptance check.** With synthetic inputs, 80 vaccinations among 100 eligible people displays as 80%. Changing the denominator to an estimate of 125 displays 64% and names the new source. Distinguish zero reported vaccinations, a missing facility report, a zero denominator and a cell suppressed for confidentiality. A suppressed cell must stay protected in the accessible table, totals, export and drill-down. The data publisher supplies disclosure-controlled outputs and handles inference from totals. [ONS disclosure control for birth and death statistics](https://www.ons.gov.uk/methodology/methodologytopicsandstatisticalconcepts/disclosurecontrol/policyonprotectingconfidentialityintablesofbirthanddeathstatistics)

### 4. Extend registration beyond a known national identity

After repairing the search journey, add examples of a patient known locally, a patient with one recorded name, a partial birth date and a person arriving under a provisional identity. Present the identifier’s issuer and verification state beside the value. Use the service’s locally permitted identifying details to disambiguate people. Keep possible duplicates available for human review and preserve the earlier identity in authorized history after reconciliation. Lily already supports partial dates and accessible duplicate comparison. [Healthcare dates guidance](../../patterns/healthcare/index.md#dates-and-times) · [Lily national identifiers](../../../spec/national-identifiers/index.md) · [Health New Zealand identity guidance](https://www.healthnz.govt.nz/health-professionals/guidance-standards/topic/health-identity/national-health-index-nhi/guidelines-for-using-the-nhi)

**Acceptance check.** A registrar can locate a synthetic patient with one name and a year of birth without supplying a fictional surname, day or national number. A patient with two issuer-specific numbers remains one person. Two patients with the same name remain separate. Read the mixed-script name and Latin identifier in an Arabic interface with a local reviewer and an assistive-technology user, then record what they heard. Keep translations used for clinical validation distinct from unreviewed machine translations. [Internationalization specification](../../../spec/internationalization/index.md) · [Screen-reader matrix](../../screen-reader-matrix.md)

### 5. Make consent, representation and access history intelligible

Add a patient-facing composition using the banner, a summary list and a timeline. In a synthetic case, a representative shares selected documents with a clinic for a stated purpose and period. On review and confirmation show who is acting, whose records are involved, the recipient, the scope and the effective dates. Let the service supply which choices and access-history details the person can see. Where an organization is the only known viewer, display that organization. The Indian, Finnish and Australian models in the comparison above give different reasons to need these fields.

**Acceptance check.** Switch from the person’s own record to a dependent’s record. The accessible heading and confirmation identify the dependent. Revoking future access leaves earlier authorized access events in history. For a service whose policy conceals even the existence of a record, the UI receives neither the entry nor its count, category or identifying attributes. Qualify Lily’s guidance about showing a count of withheld entries to cover that case. [Healthcare records guidance](../../patterns/healthcare/index.md#records-notes-and-their-provenance) · [Privacy guidance](../../../PHI.md)

## Clinical certification and assurance

Lily already publishes an assurance pack, worked hazards and permanent healthcare requirement IDs with test references. The opportunity is to connect that evidence to a **specific release, use case and observed task outcome**. The following programmes illustrate why the adopting organization needs to name its jurisdiction and product scope. [Assurance pack](../../assurance/index.md) · [Worked hazard log](../../assurance/hazard-log-example.md) · [Requirements register](../../../spec/healthcare-ui/requirements.md)

| Jurisdiction and scope                                      | What an adopter must establish for its setting                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | What Lily could supply                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| United States · ONC Health IT Certification Program         | The safety-enhanced design criterion, 45 CFR § 170.315(g)(3), addresses user-centred design and usability testing for the applicable capabilities presented for certification. [Official test method](https://healthit.gov/test-method/safety-enhanced-design/)                            | A versioned task script, expected patient context, observed use errors and exact component release for an adopter’s usability study.     |
| England · clinical risk management                          | DCB0129 addresses the manufacturer’s clinical risk management. DCB0160 addresses the health organization deploying and using the system. [NHS England standards](https://digital.nhs.uk/services/clinical-safety/clinical-risk-management-standards)                                       | For each hazard, separate the display control Lily can test from the service behaviour and residual risk that need a named local owner.  |
| Brazil · SBIS electronic health record system certification | SBIS distinguishes outpatient, inpatient and urgent-care modalities within the electronic patient record category. [SBIS categories and modalities](https://sbis.org.br/certificacoes/certificacao-software/categorias-e-modalidades-certificaveis/)                                       | Tag example evidence by care setting, role, language and activity so an adopter can select relevant records.                             |
| Finland · Kanta-connected information systems               | The system’s purpose, class and profile determine applicable requirements. Kanta describes joint testing and an information security assessment in the certification process. [Kanta certification guidance](https://www.kanta.fi/en/system-developers/certification-and-key-requirements) | Record the tested adapter/profile version and which behaviours were simulated. The supplier supplies evidence from its connected system. |

**Start with one evidence record for the search defect.** Link the worked wrong-patient hazard to the affected journey, proposed control, package or commit, test result, date, locale, browser and assistive technology. Include who performed the task, what they observed, remaining risk and the party responsible. Publish a failed or unobserved result as such. Ask a registrar and a screen-reader user to follow the duplicate-name path. A component-level accessibility check and an observed clinical task answer different assurance questions. Certification decisions belong to the applicable programme and product. [Lily assurance gaps](../../assurance/index.md) · [Manual session guidance](../../screen-reader-session.md)

**Certificates issued in care have a separate UI need.** WHO’s digital COVID-19 certificate specification distinguishes an issuer, a holder and a verifier. If an adopter needs vaccination or test certificates, prototype a read-only result showing subject, issuer, document type, verification result and time checked. When verification is unavailable, make that state visible. A “signed” provenance label alone cannot establish that verification succeeded. The issuing and verifying services own signature and trust decisions. Defer a reusable component until an adopter can supply both successful and failed verification examples. [WHO certificate concepts](https://smart.who.int/ddcc/concepts.html) · [Lily provenance contract](../../../components/record-provenance-view/index.md)

## Delivery order and field validation

| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| First change                       | Search-to-summary repair and its assurance record                           | Every fixture resolves to the selected person on initial render, reload and navigation. An unknown key reveals no record. Record the manual duplicate-name result separately.                                                                          |
| First hospital pilot               | Referral, bed transfer and post-discharge result across the existing boards | A clerk and a receiving clinician independently identify the current patient, location and outstanding action owner, including after a rejected transfer. | First community and district pilot | Interrupted home visit and an immunization coverage report                  | A community worker distinguishes saved from received. A district analyst can reproduce the rate, explain a missing report and identify which follow-up list they may open. Record task completion and unresolved ambiguity in their working languages. |
| Next patient-facing pilot          | Provisional registration and delegated record access                        | A registrar and a patient representative can tell whose record is active and what was disclosed. Repeat at phone width and with the locally used assistive technology.                                                                                 |

Start with composed examples and adopter guidance. Propose a new headless contract only when two workflows need the same semantics and the existing components cannot express them. The hospital, community and patient pilots are recommendations for future work. This audit used public primary sources, local source and test inspection, and the one server-rendering check described above. It includes no interviews, live clinical deployments, independent accessibility observations or certification tests. Local clinicians, patients, administrators and programme owners should validate the wording, responsibilities and transitions before use in care.
