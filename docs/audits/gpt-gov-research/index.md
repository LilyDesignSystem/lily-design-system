# Government and civil-society software audit of Lily Design System™

26 November 2026 · Repository baseline `49840e43b`

**Start with a resident reporting a broken streetlight.** It is a small, testable journey that matters to a municipality, a resident and a nonprofit operating a reporting service. Can someone describe the location without a map, see who will receive their name, obtain a usable reference, and return to find out whether the repair happened? Lily™ already has the address, form, review and timeline components. What an adopter needs is an example that connects them and shows what happens when an agency hands the report to someone else. [FixMyStreet citizen walkthrough](https://fixmystreet.org/training/citizens/) · [Lily government migration guide](../../migrating/government-design-system.md)

Two smaller changes should precede that pilot. Make the official-site banner example operable when copied into React, and correct the currency input’s claim about international number entry. The first affects whether residents can inspect a service’s identity. The second affects what amount a multilingual donation or grants form captures. Both behaviours were checked locally.

## Recommended patterns and field checks

### 1. A resident report, receipt and right of reply

Build the streetlight pilot from the existing address group, error summary, check-your-answers screen and timeline. Offer a landmark or written description as well as a map pin. Preview what the public will see and what the council will receive. At submission, retain the person’s entered details until the service confirms receipt. The receipt should name the responsible office, a reference, the expected next communication and an assisted contact route. Include a transfer and a resident who reports that the repair did not work. Extend the same pattern to a benefit application with its own eligibility, payout and review terminology. The host determines which remedies exist and their deadlines. [Address input](../../../components/address-input/index.md) · [Form validation](../../patterns/form-validation/index.md) · [Timeline list](../../../components/timeline-list/index.md)

**Field check.** A resident without a postal code or email can complete the scenario through a supported channel and later find the responsible office. A public report hides the resident’s private contact details in every public representation. A failed email leaves the case open, records the delivery outcome and offers the service’s alternative channel. Track successful status lookups and misread receipts in the pilot.

### 2. A fieldworker’s queue and a district population report

Show a household interview through draft, finalized on device, received by the service and returned for correction. Give each state words, a timestamp and a next action. In the district view, use a statistic, a map and an equivalent table. Name the unit, geography, collection period, data source, denominator and most recent synchronization. Offer a low-bandwidth table independently of map tiles. Include zero, unavailable, awaiting upload and suppressed values. The statistics office supplies the disclosure-controlled data, including totals and exports. [Repeatable fieldset](../../../components/repeatable-fieldset/index.md) · [Statistic](../../../components/statistic/index.md) · [Choropleth chart](../../../components/choropleth-chart/index.md)

**Field check.** For a synthetic district with 80 responding housing units out of 100 in scope, the table reports 80% and identifies the units. Ten finalized interviews on an offline device leave the published figure unchanged. A new official denominator of 125 changes the rate to 64% and exposes the revision date. A district with an unavailable return stays distinguishable from a district reporting zero. An NGO coordinator can switch to a separate organization-presence measure without carrying over the household denominator.

### 3. Public participation and election information

Create a consultation example that follows one proposal through submission, moderation, publication, support, decision and delivery. Show who made the decision, the rationale and what happens next, including for a proposal the authority declined. Alongside it, demonstrate an election information page with a locality search, a text list of sites, local opening times, language provision and accessibility details. Keep any sample ballot visibly labelled as practice through completion. For results, pair the authority’s result status with the count, coverage and update time. Local election officials own the electoral rules, official data and declaration. [Lily address input](../../../components/address-input/index.md) · [Status tag](../../../components/status-tag/index.md) · [Government identifier](../../../components/government-identifier/index.md)

**Field check.** A voter can find an accessible location and its hours using only a keyboard, with geolocation denied. A translated or printed page retains the locality, election and date. A practice user can state whether anything was cast. With a synthetic partial count, the highest displayed total must not trigger a winner label. A later declaration is attributed to the election authority. Pilot the information page first; electoral casting and certification sit with the electoral system.

### 4. Volunteer assignments and NGO casework

Compose a public opportunity page and a separate coordinator roster. Show the task, host, accessibility arrangements, expenses if offered, time zone, capacity and the volunteer’s own status. Include an open-ended translation task alongside a fixed shift. An NGO handling reports also needs an explicit queue for verification, referral and publication, with a safe public preview. A volunteer’s willingness to cover a shift and an organization’s acceptance of responsibility are different facts. [Calendar day table](../../../components/calendar-day-table/index.md) · [Existing booking flow](../../patterns/book-an-appointment.md) · [Uchaguzi publication rules](https://docs.ushahidi.com/uchaguzi-support/guidelines/privacy-and-security-guidelines)

**Field check.** Two applicants request the last place. After the host updates capacity, one sees a confirmation and the other sees the actual alternative, such as a waiting list. Withdrawal updates the roster after acknowledgment. A remote volunteer can accept flexible work without inventing a shift time. A public preview of a community report excludes private contact details and the precise location whenever the publication policy requires it.

### 5. A funding ledger and an evidence page for grants

Use the currency-entry fix in a nonprofit pledge flow. Show original currency, pledge schedule, amount received and remaining balance. For public grants, name the funder, recipient where publishable, decision date, purpose and whether funds were recommended, awarded or disbursed. On a separate evidence page show the proposed outcome, comparator, time horizon, model version, source and material uncertainties. Where reviewers choose how to weight outcomes, disclose whose judgments the model uses. Government funders can add statutory aims and distributional considerations. Lily should display the funder’s criteria and leave the weighting decision with the funder. [Currency input](../../../components/currency-input/index.md) · [Graphic block](../../../components/graphic-block/index.md) · [GiveWell’s use and limits of estimates](https://www.givewell.org/how-we-work/our-criteria/cost-effectiveness)

**Field check.** A synthetic EUR 100 pledge with one EUR 25 payment shows EUR 25 received and EUR 75 outstanding. A grant recommendation adds nothing to cash disbursed until a payment record exists. A modelled estimate carries its version and units in the chart, accessible table and downloadable record. Two estimates with incompatible outcomes or time horizons should prompt an explanation before a reader compares them.

| Partner pilots      | Consultation decisions, volunteer assignments and grant evidence.                                               | A community organizer, volunteer coordinator and grant reviewer test their own terminology, exception cases and privacy rules before a shared component API is proposed.       |

Keep the first implementation in composed examples and the adopter guide. Reuse Lily’s existing migration checks at 320 CSS pixels, high zoom, keyboard-only operation and in a locally used right-to-left language. Ask fluent participants to review translated status messages, including what is spoken after a submission. Across pilots, the service owns identity verification, authorization, storage, delivery, counts, funding decisions and electoral administration. Lily owns markup, focus, visible and spoken status, and examples that represent the service’s evidence accurately. [Government migration checks](../../migrating/government-design-system.md) · [Label packs](../../../spec/label-packs/index.md) · [Lily accessibility evidence](../../accessibility-statement.md)

**Scope of evidence.** This was a purposive review of primary public documentation and Lily source, plus two interaction checks against the local React build using a simulated DOM. The proposed field checks use invented data. No production service, election, payment, assistive-technology session or resident interview was run as part of this audit. The recommendations are prioritized by the consequence of a misleading display and the opportunity to reuse a pattern across agencies and civil society.
