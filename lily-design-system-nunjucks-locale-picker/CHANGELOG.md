# Changelog — @lilydesignsystem/nunjucks-locale-picker

Changes to this package, newest first. The monorepo's [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/LilyDesignSystem/lily-design-system/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) records every change to the whole
system, with its reasoning.

## 0.3.1 — 2026-10-21

- Wu Chinese (`wuu`) and Nigerian Pidgin (`pcm`) are named in their own languages, 吴语 and Naijá, not by an `Intl` fallback name.
- The `*-gb` locales are named for Great Britain, not the United Kingdom.
