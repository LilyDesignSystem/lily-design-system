Shigarwa

Lily tana zaune a monorepo na Git ɗaya, mai kundi ga ɗakin karatu marar kai na kowane tsarin aiki, fakitin masu taimako da aikace-aikacen misali. Hanya mafi sauri ta gwada ita ce kwafa monorepo ka buɗe ɗakin karatu marar kai don tarinka: 

git clone https://github.com/LilyDesignSystem/lily-design-system
cd lily-design-system/lily-design-system-react-headless
pnpm install

Tsari iri ɗaya yana aiki ga sauran tsarin aiki:

  • @lilydesignsystem/html-headless — ba a buƙatar shigarwa; kwafi .html fayiloli
  • @lilydesignsystem/svelte-headless — pnpm install
  • @lilydesignsystem/react-headless — pnpm install
  • @lilydesignsystem/vue-headless — pnpm install
  • @lilydesignsystem/angular-headless — pnpm install
  • @lilydesignsystem/nunjucks-headless — pnpm install
  • lily-design-system-blazor-headless — dotnet build

An tsara ɗakunan karatu marasa kai don a kwafa ko a ɗora cikin aiki — ɗauki tushen lamba a matsayin tushen gaskiya ka kwafi abin da kake buƙata. masu taimakon zaɓi ƙari suna zuwa a matsayin fakiti da za a iya shigarwa tare da bututun gini da bugawa (npm don tsarin aikin JS, NuGet don Blazor).

Yi amfani da ɓangare marar kai

Abubuwa marasa kai suna kawo HTML mai ma’ana, ARIA da kadarori — amma ba CSS ba. Ga Button a kowane tsarin aiki:

HTML

<button class="button" type="button" aria-label="Save">
  Save
</button>

Svelte

<script>
  import Button from "lily-design-system-svelte-headless/components/Button/Button.svelte";
</script>

<Button onclick={save}>Save</Button>

React

import Button from "lily-design-system-react-headless/components/Button";

<Button onClick={save}>Save</Button>

Vue

<script setup>
  import Button from "lily-design-system-vue-headless/components/Button.vue";
</script>

<Button @click="save">Save</Button>

Angular

import { Button } from "@lilydesignsystem/angular-headless";

@Component({
  imports: [Button],
  template: `<lily-button (click)="save()">Save</lily-button>`,
})

Blazor

<Button OnClick="Save">Save</Button>

Nunjucks

{% from "components/button/macro.njk" import button %}

{{ button({ text: "Save", type: "button" }) }}

Yi amfani da misali mai ado

Aikace-aikacen misali sun haɗa da CSS, hanyoyi, da cikakkun shafukan nuni. Hanya mafi sauri ta gwaji ita ce fara aikace-aikacen misali na SvelteKit, Next, Nuxt, Analog ko Eleventy ka duba nunin a /components. 

git clone https://github.com/LilyDesignSystem/lily-design-system
cd lily-design-system/lily-design-system-svelte-sveltekit-examples
pnpm install
pnpm run dev

Sai ka buɗe http://localhost:5173 ka bincika /components.

Ado da alamomin zane

Kowane ɓangare ana nuna shi da aji ɗaya na kebab-case a kan tushen abunsa. Misali, <Button> ana nuna shi a matsayin <button class="button">. Yi masa ado yadda kake so: 

.button {
  background: var(--my-primary);
  color: #fff;
  padding: 0.75rem 1.5rem;
  border-radius: 0.5rem;
}
.button:hover { background: var(--my-primary-hover); }

Launukan Lily na tsoho (da ake amfani da su a aikace-aikacen misali) su ne:

  • Na farko: #2563eb
  • Haɗari: #dc2626
  • Gargaɗi: #f59e0b
  • Nasara: #16a34a
  • Bangon shafi: #f9fafb
  • Bangon kati: #ffffff

Waɗannan shawarwari ne, ba buƙatu ba. Maye gurbinsu da launukan alamar kasuwancinka kuma Lily tana bi cikin farin ciki.

Jigogi da aka shirya

Ba ka son rubuta CSS daga farko? Lily tana kawo jigogi 57 na tunani masu zaman kansu a kundin themes/ . Kowanne takardar salo ɗaya ce da ke nufin ƙugiyoyin aji na Lily — haɗa ta kuma an yi maka ado: 

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/assets/themes/united-kingdom-government-digital-service.css" />

Saitin yana rufe:

  • Fannin gwamnati — NHS England, NHS Scotland da NHS Wales (kowanne a salo na marasa lafiya da na ma’aikata), GOV.UK GDS, da U.S. Web Design System.
  • Wanda masu sayarwa suka ƙarfafa — Adobe Spectrum, Mozilla Protocol.
  • Manufa ta gama-gari — light, dark, nord, dracula, wireframe, da kusan talatin ƙari.

Masu zaɓen jigo suna amfani da :where(...), don haka CSS ɗinka koyaushe yana yin nasara a takamaimai. Haɗa su da mai taimakon theme-picker da ke ƙasa don sauyawa lokacin gudu, ko bi koyarwar jigo.

Masu taimakon zaɓi

Kowane tsarin aiki yana da ƙananan masu zaɓe takwas, kowanne fakiti na lily-design-system-{framework}-{package} nasa (da picker-bar da ke haɗa su). Kowanne maɓallin gunki ne marar kai da ke buɗe tagar fitowa — akwatin jeri, jerin hanyoyi, fom ɗin bincike, ko (don date-time-picker) tattaunawar zaɓen kwanan wata — ba na asali ba <select>; yana da amintaccen SSR; kuma ba ya kawo CSS:

  • theme-picker — yana ɗora takardun salo na jigo lokacin gudu ta hanyar canza <link>da ake sarrafawa, yana saita data-theme a kan takarda, kuma zaɓi yana adana a localStorage.
  • locale-picker — yana saita lang da dir (tare da gano RTL na atomatik) don ɗakin karatunka na i18n ya bi; shi kansa ba ya fassara komai.
  • text-size-picker — yana saita data-text-size a kan takarda; CSS ɗinka yana daidaita kowace ƙima da girman rubutu.
  • motion-picker — yana saita data-motion a kan takarda, yana farawa daga saitin rage motsi na baƙo; CSS da rubutun aikinka suna yanke shawarar abin da za a danne.
  • search-picker — maɓallin gunki da ke buɗe filin bincike; miƙawa yana zuwa shafin bincike. Yana mallakar aiki, ba zaɓi ba.
  • link-picker — gunkin gida da ke buɗe menu na hanyoyin shafuka da aikace-aikacenka ya ayyana (Gida, Game da Mu, Tuntuɓe Mu, Manufar Sirri, …). Yana mallakar aiki, ba zaɓi ba.
  • share-picker — yana buɗe takardar rabawa ta asali ko jerin wuraren da mai amfani ya bayar, tare da kwafin URL. Yana mallakar aiki, ba zaɓi ba: ba ya amfani da komai, ba ya adana komai.
  • date-time-picker — filin rubutu tare da tattaunawar zaɓen kwanan wata ta APG don kwanan wata, lokaci, ko duka biyu. Yana mallakar ƙimar fom, ba zaɓi ba: zaren ISO yana juyawa gaba da baya zuwa <input type="date">. 
git clone https://github.com/LilyDesignSystem/lily-design-system
cd lily-design-system/lily-design-system-svelte-theme-picker

Jerin Svelte shi ne ma’anar ƙa’ida; tashoshin React, Vue, Angular, HTML, Nunjucks, Web Components da Blazor suna daidaita da shi yarjejeniya-da-yarjejeniya. Duba koyarwar masu taimako.

Samun dama

Abubuwan suna nufin WCAG 2.2 AAA. Suna bin waɗannan tsare-tsare:

  • Abubuwan HTML masu ma’ana maimakon na gama-gari <div>s.
  • <label for="id"> masu haɗa lakabi da shigarwa.
  • aria-labelledby / aria-describedby don nassoshi na giciye.
  • aria-invalid + aria-errormessage don yanayin kuskure.
  • role="alert" da aria-live don abun ciki mai canzawa.
  • aria-pressed, aria-expanded, aria-current don yanayi.
  • Roving tabindex don grid.

Mai amfani ne ke ba da alamomin mayar da hankali da gangan — Lily ba ta taɓa zana zoben mayar da hankali na tsoho da ya saɓa wa zanenka ba. Aikace-aikacen misali suna riƙe tsabtataccen tushen axe-core a duk hanyoyinsu.

Daidaita ƙasashen duniya

Kowace lakabi, mai riƙe wuri, saƙon kuskure da rubutun maɓalli kadara ce. Babu rubutun da aka rubuta a cikin lamba. Ɗora tsarin fassarar da ka zaɓa — Paraglide, i18next, vue-i18n, react-intl, fayilolin .resx, kowane.

Don kwanan wata, lambobi da kuɗaɗe, abubuwan suna karɓar zaren da aka riga aka tsara: kai ne ke tsara da Intl.DateTimeFormat / Intl.NumberFormat / ɗakin karatunka da ka fi so ka miƙa sakamakon.

Mai taimakon locale-picker yana sanar da yaren da aka zaɓa ga ɗakin karatunka na i18n ta hanyar saita lang da dir a kan tushen takarda.

Gwaji

Kowane ƙaramin aikin tsarin aiki yana kawo gwaje-gwajensa ta amfani da tarinsa na al’ada:

  • HTML: WebDriverIO da ke gudanar da ainihin masu bincike.
  • Svelte: Vitest + @testing-library/svelte.
  • React: Vitest + @testing-library/react.
  • Vue: Vitest + @testing-library/vue.
  • Angular: Vitest + TestBed (ta hanyar plugin ɗin Analog Vite).
  • Nunjucks: Vitest tare da mai taimakon nuna.
  • Blazor: bUnit.

Gwaje-gwajen suna amfani da masu daidaita Vitest na ciki kawai — ba taɓa jest-dom masu daidaita ba. Wannan yana sa gwaje-gwajen su zama masu ɗaukuwa. Aikace-aikacen misali suna ƙara gwaje-gwajen Playwright daga ƙarshe zuwa ƙarshe, tushen samun dama na axe-core, da duba wuraren duba masu amsawa.

Wakilan AI

Wannan shafi yana buga llms.txt da llms.json a tushensa — taswirar da aka zaɓa ta manyan shafukansa, ga kowane kayan aiki da ke bin al’adar llms.txt.

Monorepo na ƙa’ida kuma yana kawo Claude Skills: lily-design-system-skill guda biyu: ƙwarewar manufa ta gama-gari da ke rufe ra’ayoyin Lily, kalmomi da tsare-tsaren haɗawa ga duk wanda ke gini da tsarin, da lily-design-system-maintainer-skill, ƙwarewar fasaha da ke rufe tsarin fayilolin da ake buƙata na monorepo da kayan aiki ga duk wanda ke aiki a kai. Dukansu suna a tushen monorepo na ƙa’ida.

Magance matsaloli

Ɓangare yana nunawa amma yana kama marar ado

Wannan marar kai ne yana aiki kamar yadda aka nufa — babu CSS da ke zuwa da ɓangaren. Ko dai rubuta CSS don ajin kebab-case na ɓangaren (da ake nunawa a kowane shafin jeri) ko haɗa ɗaya daga cikin jigogi 57 da aka shirya.

CSS ɗina bai bayyana yana aiki a kan jigo ba

Ya kamata — masu zaɓen jigo an naɗe su a :where(...), wanda ke da takamaimai sifili. Idan doka tana ci gaba da asara, duba cewa takardar salonka tana ɗorawa bayan <link> na jigo kuma mai zaɓen yana daidaita da gaske da ƙugiyar ajin ɓangaren.

pnpm install yana kasawa da kuskuren peer ko sigar

Yi amfani da pnpm na yanzu (v10+) da Node 22+. Kowane ƙaramin aiki yana ƙulla sigogin tsarin aikinsa a package.jsonnasa; idan sarkar kayan aikinka ta duniya ta fi tsufa, pnpm env use --global lts shi ne gyara mafi sauri.

Mai karanta allo yana sanar da sunan da bai dace ba ga abin sarrafawa

Duba kadarar da ake buƙata ta label ta ɓangaren — abubuwan da ba su da rubutu mai gani suna buƙatar ɗaya, kuma aria-label / aria-labelledby da mai amfani ya bayar aka miƙa ta rest-props da gangan suna yin nasara a kan haɗin da ke ciki. Kowane shafin jeri yana rubuta yarjejeniyar ARIA ta ɓangaren.

Mai taimakon theme-picker ba ya canza takardun salo

Tabbatar cewa themesUrl yana nuna kundin da mai bincike zai iya ɗauko (bayar da fayilolin themes/ a matsayin kadarori na tsaye) kuma cewa sunayen jigo a kadararka ta themes suna daidaita da sunayen fayiloli. Mai taimakon yana canza href na <link data-lily-theme-picker> ɗaya da ake sarrafawa — duba shi a kayan aikin mai haɓakawa don ganin URL da ake nema.

FAQ

Me ya sa marar kai maimakon mai ado?

Abubuwan da aka riga aka yi wa ado suna da sauƙi — har sai sun ƙi dacewa da alamar kasuwancinka. Abubuwa marasa kai suna buƙatar ɗan ƙarin aiki a farko amma suna ba ka cikakken iko kan zanen gani. Aikace-aikacen misali da jigogi 57 suna nuna hanyoyin yi musu ado; za ka iya ɗaukar su ko ka maye gurbinsu gaba ɗaya. Dogon hujjar tana a Dalilin Lily.

Me ya sa abubuwa da yawa haka?

Lily tana nufin rufe tsare-tsaren da yawancin aikace-aikace ke buƙata ba tare da tilasta maka gina su daga farko ba — har da abubuwa masu zurfi kamar shigarwar masu gano na ƙasa da scrollytelling na edita. Jerin yana ɗaukowa daga tsarin zane goma sha biyu da aka kafa tare da aikin asali — duba Game da.

Zan iya amfani da Lily da Tailwind?

Eh. Kowane ɓangare yana bayyana aji ɗaya na tushe na kebab-case tare da className / classda mai amfani ya bayar. Shimfiɗa kayan aikin Tailwind a sama yadda kake so.

Zan iya amfani da Lily da tsarin CSS masu ma’ana kamar DaisyUI?

Eh. Sunayen aji na kebab-case a kan tushen abu suna aiki a matsayin ƙugiyoyin CSS masu ma’ana. Haɗa Lily da tsari mai ma’ana kuma za ka sami abubuwan da aka riga aka yi wa ado waɗanda har yanzu suke girmama ARIA da i18n.

Akwai fakitin npm?

Masu taimakon zaɓi suna zuwa a matsayin fakiti tare da bututun bugawa na npm/NuGet. An tsara ɗakunan karatu na abubuwa marasa kai don a kwafa ko a ɗora cikin aiki — tushen lamba shi ne abin da ake kawowa, don haka za ka iya karantawa, rage, da mallakar ainihin abin da kake aikawa. Buga ɗakunan karatu marasa kai a rajista har yanzu yana kan taswirar hanya.

Me ya sa lasisi da yawa?

Ayyuka daban-daban suna da buƙatun lasisi daban-daban. BSD da MIT masu ba da izini ne, Apache-2.0 yana da bayar da haƙƙin mallaka, kuma zaɓukan GPL suna goyon bayan copyleft. Zaɓi wanda ya dace da yanayinka.
