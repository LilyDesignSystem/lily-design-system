Ƙwarewa a kan wakilan AI suna sa Lily ta fi sauƙi

Lily tana kawo ƙwarewa mai mayar da hankali — ta gama-gari, ta kowane tsarin aiki, da ta kowane ƙaramin aiki — don wakili ya ɗora daidai mahallin da yake buƙata kuma babu abin da ba ya buƙata.

Wace ƙwarewa kake so?

Kowane mataki yana amsa tambaya daban. Ɗora ƙwarewar gama-gari idan ba ka san Lily ba tukuna. Ɗora ƙwarewar tsarin aiki idan ka san tsarin aikinka amma ba ka san wane ƙaramin aiki kake buƙata ba. Ɗora headless-skill ko helpers-skill kai tsaye idan ka riga ka san ainihin abin da kake aiki a kai.

  • Gama-gari / mai kulawa — “Menene Lily?” / “Yaya aka gina wannan monorepo?” — lily-design-system-skill, lily-design-system-maintainer-skill
  • Laimar tsarin aiki — “Me ke akwai don {framework}, kuma wane ɓangare nake buƙata?” — 8, {framework}-skill ɗaya kowanne
  • Marar kai / masu taimako — “Ta yaya zan yi amfani da abubuwan / masu taimakon *-picker na {framework} daidai?” — 16, {framework}-headless-skill ɗaya da {framework}-helpers-skill ɗaya kowanne
