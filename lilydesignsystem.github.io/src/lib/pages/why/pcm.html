Start from your design

Your team don already know how your product suppose look. Lily start exactly from there: e dey supply di parts wey really hard to do well and costly to do again — di correct semantic element, di ARIA states, di keyboard contract, focus management — and leave every visual decision completely, joyfully, for you. Your CSS na di look of di design system, from day one.

Because every component carry stable class hook and pure structure, your styling go stay cleanly yours through every upgrade — and di accessibility wey dey under am go keep working, version after version.

Wetin Lily promise

  • Accessible by default. WCAG 2.2 AAA na di target for every component. Patterns dey follow di WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices; every interactive component fit work with keyboard with documented contract; di example apps dey keep clean axe-core baseline.
  • Internationalizable by construction. Every string wey user go see — each label, error, and announcement — na prop wey get stable names for all frameworks, so translation go flow naturally. RTL and bidirectional text dey inherit from your dir. Locale-aware components dey take di locale as prop, always explicitly.
  • One catalog, eight frameworks. Di same 571 components (536 as native Web Components) with di same class hooks and prop conventions for HTML, Svelte, React, Vue, Angular, Blazor, Nunjucks, and Web Components. Your CSS and your team knowledge go move across stacks.
  • CSS-strategy agnostic. One stable kebab-case class for every component root. Semantic CSS, Tailwind utilities, CSS-in-JS, or one of di 57 ready-made themes — Lily welcome all of dem and e go gracefully comot for your way.
  • Free, forever, for your own terms. Open source under your choice of BSD, MIT, Apache-2.0, GPL-2.0, or GPL-3.0. No pro tier, no seat licenses, no telemetry.

Depth where e matter

Beyond di usual buttons and dialogs, di catalog reach pass most:

  • Public-sector patterns wey dem research from di NHS UK, GOV.UK, USWDS, and ONS design systems — error summaries, warning callouts, summary lists, phase banners, skip links.
  • 140 national personal identifier components wey cover healthcare, national-ID, tax, and passport identifiers across 50+ countries, each one dey document im format and validation algorithm.
  • Editorial and scrollytelling primitives wey dem adapt from Reuters Graphics — article layouts, scrollers, timelines, tile maps.
  • Picker helpers — small packages wey each one own one interaction from start to finish: theme switching (with runtime stylesheet loading), language, text size, reduced motion, search, page links, sharing, and date-time entry. Each one na headless icon button wey open popup — no be native <select> — with persistence and SSR safety wey dey inside.

Themes for di public sector, ready from di box
