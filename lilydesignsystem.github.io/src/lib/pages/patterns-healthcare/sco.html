Healthcare uiser interfaces

A guide fur teams biggin clinical saftware wi Lily: clinical portals, results an reportin, prescribin an medicines administration, decision support an hospital dashboards.

This owersettin is a machine draft that naebody has checkit yet. The English page is the ane that coonts.

Whit Lily daes, an whit it daesnae

Lily gies ye display contracts: semantic markup, ARIA, keyboard behaviour an class hooks fur the things clinical screens repeat. It daesnae gie ye clinical logic, clinical content or a regulatory claim.

Regulation. Saftware that works oot a score, a centile, a dose or a drug interaction is usually a medical device. Lily works oot nane o thae, on purpose. If yer product is regulatit, ye ain its usability engineerin file (IEC 62366-1, the FDA's human factors guidance, EU MDR). Lily's tests, its self-assessit accessibility conformance report an its screen-reader matrix can be evidence in that file; nane o them is a conformance claim, an the matrix haes nae results yet.

Alerts: interrupt anely fur whit's critical

Alert fatigue is a safety problem: whan ilka alert interrupts, fowk dismiss them aw. AHRQ's guidance is tae tier alerts bi severity an mak anely the severe anes interruptive.

  • Information (indicator="info"): inline, aside the task, a decision-support caird in the page.
  • Warnin (indicator="warning"): veesible athoot blockin, the same caird, stylit tae staund oot; nae modal.
  • Critical (indicator="critical"): pit the caird inside an alert dialog, an offer overrideReasons sae dismissin it asks fur a reason.
  • A decision-support caird is niver a live region bi itsel. Announce a new critical caird bi movin focus intae the dialog, no bi aria-live.
  • Tak awa a caird whan the event it answert happens again, an record ilka ootcome (accepit, or owerridden wi its reason).

Wrang-patient errors: identify, dinnae restrict

  • Shaw twa identifiers wherever a patient's data appears: the name wi the date o birth, an the national identifier wi its view component (The Joint Commission, Quick Safety 45).
  • Uise the patient banner on ilka screen that acts on ae patient's record, an keep it veesible.
  • Afore an action on a record ither than the ane in the banner, confirm it wi the name, date o birth an identifier.
  • Dinnae limit hou mony records a clinician can open: a randomised trial o 3,356 clinicians (JAMA, 2019) fand that it didnae reduce wrang-patient orders, an fowk wirk roond it.

The patient banner

The composition is documentit wi medical-banner, wi a wirkin page at /patient-banner in the SvelteKit example app:

  • twa identifiers, an the age aside the date o birth;
  • allergies throu an allergy list, sae "nae kent allergies", "no recordit" an "unavailable" niver luik alike;
  • a missin value is wirds ("No kent"), niver a question mark, a dash or an toom cell;
  • a deceased patient: data-deceased fur the theme, an a veesible line o text, niver colour alane;
  • nae field cuttit short, clippit or hidden at ony width; it wraps.

Results

  • Ae display fur a result, whitever system it cam frae: the observation view.
  • The flag is wirds frae yer translation (flagLabel), wi the FHIR interpretation code as data-interpretation fur the theme.
  • Separate new results frae earlier anes, an shaw a trend (a sparkline, or a line chart wi its data table): clinicians luik fur a significant chynge, no jist the range.
  • A result that wis niver recordit an ane that's unavailable are different states, wi different wirds.

Medicines

  • Generic names in lawer case, except tall man letters frae the FDA an ISMP lists or yer country's (Australia an Canada publish thair ain). Mairk them in nameSegments; Lily niver guesses them.
  • Scrieve the strength wi formatQuantity: nae trailin zero, a leadin zero, an a no-brak space afore the unit.
  • Scrieve mL, niver cc, an avoid ilka abbreviation on ISMP's error-prone list.
  • Niver cut short a medicine's name or dose.

Dates an times

  • Scrieve the month as text sae a date cannae be read twa weys (Intl.DateTimeFormat wi month: "short").
  • Pick the clock on purpose: pass hourCycle explicitly. The NHS asks fur the 24-hour clock; that's a UK rule, no a warldwide ane, sae Lily niver picks it fur ye.
  • Ages follae the Common User Interface bands throu ageParts(); a service wi anither convention passes its ain wirds.

Dashboards

  • A ward board or command-centre screen updates aften. Niver announce ilka chynge: keep ae couthie summary region ("3 beds free, 2 discharges due") an lat fowk read the board.
  • Ilka state on a board (bed clean, occupied, blockit) is text or a data-* state wi wirds, niver colour alane.

Whit Lily will no dae

  • Work oot an early warnin score, a growth centile, a dose or a reference range.
  • Ship a drug database, a tall man list, or SNOMED CT, ICD, LOINC or UCUM codes.
  • Reproduce a licensed instrument (Wong-Baker FACES) or anither body's design (the NEWS2 chart).
  • Limit the nummer o records open at yince.
