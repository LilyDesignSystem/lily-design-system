Abin da “yana nufin WCAG 2.2 AAA” yake nufi a nan

Kalmar yana nufin an zaɓe ta da gangan. Wannan shafi yana faɗin daidai abin da ake tabbatarwa, da me, abin da babu abin da ke tabbatarwa, da abin da muke niyyar yi game da gibin. Lily ba ta taɓa iƙirarin “bin” WCAG ba — babu wani bincike da ke goyon bayan hakan.

An tabbatar akai-akai

  • Abin ma’ana na ƙa’ida, halayen ARIA da ƙugiyoyin aji na kowane ɓangare — an tabbatar da su ta hanyar gwaje-gwajen raka’a na kowane tsarin aiki (sama da yanayi 15,000 a tsarin aiki bakwai).
  • Kowanne daga cikin shafukan nuni 581 na abubuwa a aikace-aikacen misali na SvelteKit yana da tsabta ta axe (saitin dokokin WCAG 2.0/2.1 A+AA da 2.2 AA) — duba dukkan jeri, an fara kafa tushe a 2026-08-27 kuma aka sake gudanarwa a 2026-10-06 — da tushen kowane aikace-aikace don shafin gida, jeri da hanyoyin haɗaka a dukkan aikace-aikacen misali bakwai.
  • Yarjejeniyoyin madannai ga kowane ɓangare mai hulɗa, da aka rubuta a cikin bayanan ƙa’ida na kowane ɓangare kuma aka yi amfani da su ta gwaje-gwajen; fakitin masu taimako suna gudanar da ƙayyadaddun Playwright a ainihin masu bincike ƙari.
  • Mafi ƙarancin girman manufa na WCAG 2.2 da kariyar zubewa a dukkan jigogi 57; hanyar tsallakewa, alamomin wuri, kuma babu zubewa a kwance a wuraren duba huɗu ga kowane aikace-aikace; lang/dir amfani, har da juyawar RTL.

Babu abin da ya tabbatar

  • Ba a taɓa yin binciken bin ƙa’ida ba. Babu VPAT, babu takaddun shaida. Kayan aikin atomatik suna rufe ƙananan sashe na ma’aunin nasara na WCAG.
  • Ma’aunin AAA na musamman (ingantaccen bambanci da sauransu) niyyar zane ne, ba halaye da aka tabbatar ba; saitin dokokin axe suna gudana A/AA. Babu jigon bambanci mai girma na musamman tukuna.
  • Halin masu karanta allo ba a gwada shi ba da yawa. Gwaje-gwajen suna tabbatar da halayen ARIA, ba abin da VoiceOver, NVDA ko JAWS ke sanarwa ba — kuma wannan aiki ya fitar da gwaje-gwajen kore a kan ainihin lahani fiye da sau ɗaya, kowanne an rubuta a cikin changelog saboda gwaje-gwajen ba su iya ganin su ba.

Abin da muke niyyar yi game da shi

Binciken samun dama mai zaman kansa shi ne amfani na farko da aka ambata na kowane tallafin aiki, kuma iyakar da mai bincike ke buƙata ta riga ta shirya. Har zuwa lokacin, rahotannin masu karanta allo su ne gudummawar da wannan aiki ya fi so: “ɓangare X yana sanar da Y, wanda ba daidai ba ne saboda Z” ana iya yin aiki da shi kai tsaye.
